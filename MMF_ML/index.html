    Multilayer moving frames (MMF-ml: algorithm: mmf, field: lift)¶

    MMF-ml is the moving-frames tracker of MMF_TRACTOGRAPHY.md (MMF-4h: CF4 steps of advanceFrame with the hold scheme) run on a multilayer field instead of the principal eigenvector. The DTI field has one direction per voxel, and where two bundles cross that direction belongs to neither of them. MMF-ml gives every bundle through a voxel its own layer: a direction, a frame and a connection 1-form. A tract reads only the layer that continues its own heading. Everything else is unchanged: the step, the integrator, the termination, the coast through missing data and the exit test are those of MMF-4h.

    MMF-ml leaves the professor's scheme untouched. Sehun Chun's advanceFrame.m and nim_tractography_mmf.m are not edited. MMF-ml runs on copies of them, advanceFrame_ml.m and nim_tractography_mmf_ml.m, which add one thing: the connection, the frames and the confidence may be functions f(X, T) of the position and the heading. runTractography sends algorithm: mmf to the copy only for field: lift (or for the history veto, mmf.veto_arc > 0). Every other mmf run uses the originals. tests/unit/TestAdvanceFrameMl.m checks that, with neither addition in use, the copies take exactly the same steps as the originals.

    ./bin/run_tractography.sh mmf_ml --score          # config/mmf_ml.yml

    The layers are the lifts of the bundles. A bundle's direction field, lifted to the space of positions and directions, is a sheet over the brain. Code and config keys keep that name: nim_lift_field, nim_lift_connection, field: lift, tractography.lift.*.

    Notation¶

    symbol meaning domain / units
    Omega the voxel grid of the scan, nodes x in Z^3 voxel coordinates
    RP^2 lines through the origin, S^2 / {u ~ -u} (a fibre direction has no sign)
    M = Omega x RP^2 positions and fibre directions; pi(x, u) = x 5-dimensional
    g_i, b_i unit gradient direction and b-value of measurement i s/mm^2
    y_i(x) = S_i(x) / S_0(x) the attenuation at node x dimensionless
    n_j, j = 1..J dictionary directions, near-uniform on the half sphere (Fibonacci), J = lift.ndirs unit vectors
    a_j(x) >= 0 amount of fibre along n_j
    u_k(x) direction of layer k at node x, k = 1..P (P = lift.max_lifts) unit vector, sign free
    e1, e2, e3 the moving frame; e1 = tangent unit vectors
    w_ij connection 1-forms, w_ij,k = e_j . d e_i / dx_k rad / voxel
    c(X, T) read weight of the layer of heading T at the point X in [0, 1]
    CL = (l1 - l2) / l1 linear shape of the diffusion tensor (eigenvalues l1 >= l2 >= l3) in [0, 1]

    1. From the signal to the layers (nim_lift_field)¶

    Forward model. A voxel holds sticks of fibre along the n_j, plus isotropic compartments that carry no direction:

    y_i(x) = sum_j a_j(x) K(g_i, n_j) + sum_d c_d(x) exp(-b_i d)
K(g, n) = exp(-b (l_perp + (l_par - l_perp) (g . n)^2))

    l_par, l_perp are the single-fibre diffusivities: the median DTI l1 and (l2 + l3)/2 of the tissue voxels whose FA is above the lift.ref_fa_pct-th percentile. The isotropic compartments are d in {l_perp, 3e-3 mm^2/s} (lift.iso: auto), the hindered water and the free water. Without them, the stick sum would build isotropic signal out of many sticks, and those sticks would become false layers.

    Deconvolution. At every tissue node, non-negative least squares (lsqnonneg) with a prior of few layers, mu = lift.sparsity:

    min over a, c >= 0 of  || K a + K_iso c - y ||^2 + mu (sum_j a_j)^2

    The prior is one extra row of the system, sqrt(mu) (1 ... 1 0 0) against 0. It is needed because a sum of many sticks can reproduce broad signal as well as the isotropic compartments can. mu = 0.03 was the best value tried on ISMRM 2015 (see Settings).

    Orientation density. p(x, u) = sum_j a_j(x) W(u . n_j), with the Watson kernel W(t) = exp(kappa (t^2 - 1)), kappa = lift.kappa (20).

    The layers at a node are the local maxima of p(x, .):

    • Search. Maxima are found over the dictionary (12 degree neighbourhood), then refined to the continuous maximum by the fixed point of the gradient condition u <- sum_j a_j W(u . n_j)(u . n_j) n_j / | . |.
    • Kept when all of these hold:
    • p >= lift.rel x max p;
    • lobe mass >= lift.mass_min. The lobe mass m = sum_{|u.n_j| >= cos 20 deg} a_j / sum_j a_j estimates the volume fraction.
    • at least lift.sep degrees from a stronger kept layer;
    • strongest first, at most lift.max_lifts.

    Continuity through lost lobes (lift.bridge, 2 nodes). A layer's lobe can vanish for m <= 2 consecutive nodes along u while the layer continues beyond them within 20 degrees. The gap nodes then receive the mean direction of the two ends (L.filled), because fibres do not end inside white matter. On the ISMRM 2015 coronal slice y = 54, 113 of the 154 layers that are not maxima of their own node's density are such fills.

    The output is nim.lift_U [X Y Z P 3] (unit directions), nim.lift_ok [X Y Z P] (which exist) and nim.lift_mass. nim_field builds them once and caches them next to the nim. The cache is <nim>_lift_<hash>.mat, where the hash covers every setting that changes the layers, the version of nim_lift_field and the nim file.

    2. The connection of each layer (nim_lift_connection)¶

    The connection is computed at the nodes, before tracking ("field first, then the ODE"), exactly as the nodal connection of field: dti. The difference is that it is computed per layer.

    Branch. Each layer must first be continued to its neighbours. A layer u at node x continues to x +- e_k (k = 1, 2, 3) as the layer v of that neighbour closest to u, with |u . v| >= cos 30 deg; if no layer qualifies, the branch ends. It continues to x +- 2 e_k as the layer closest to that match. This matching separates crossing bundles: their layers are more than 30 degrees apart, so a branch never jumps from one bundle to the other.

    Derivative (lift.stencil: fd4, the stencil of field: dti). The dyadic M = u u' is sign free, and on the matched branch:

    dM/dx_k = (M_-2 - 8 M_-1 + 8 M_+1 - M_+2) / 12     branch reaches +-2 on both sides
        = (M_+1 - M_-1) / 2                        it reaches +-1
        = least-squares slope over the reached t   otherwise
dU/dx_k = (dM/dx_k) u
T(a, b, k) = u_a dU_b/dx_k                         (the nodal connection tensor, sign free)

    lift.stencil: ls (the code default) uses the least-squares slope everywhere. It is smoother and reads less curvature on bends.

    3. Reading a layer at a point¶

    At the point X with incoming heading T, each of the 8 corner nodes of X contributes the layer u closest to T if its angle to T is within lift.read_tol (30 deg); a corner without one contributes nothing. With trilinear weights omega_q, summed over the contributing corners, the read weight is:

    c(X, T) = sum_{corners q that contribute} omega_q(X)                 in [0, 1]

    If c < lift.read_min (0.125) nothing is read. Otherwise the dyadic and the tensor T are interpolated with the weights renormalised over the contributing corners:

    e1      = principal axis of sum_q omega_q u_q u_q' / c,  signed along T
e2      = the Cartesian axis least aligned with e1, projected on its normal plane;  e3 = e1 x e2
w_1j,k  = e_j . sum_a e1_a T(a, :, k),  j = 2, 3;     w_23 = 0  ('Twist' 'zero')

    With one layer per voxel and u_1 = v1, this is the scheme of field: dti: MMF-ml with P = 1 is MMF-4h on DTI. lift.interp: sheet_linear | sheet_cubic reads the whole 4 x 4 x 4 neighbourhood as one sheet instead. It scored no better (0.539 against 0.540) and is off.

    4. Tracking¶

    The tract solves the moving-frames ODE of advanceFrame with the field read on its own heading:

    dx/ds = e1,   dE/ds = Omega(x, e1) E,   Omega_1j = w_1j(x, e1) . e1   (CF4, h = integrator.step)

    The hold (mmf.hold: hold). In MMF-4h the confidence is the tensor's CL. In MMF-ml it is the read weight c(X, T): a point where c < mmf.hold_min (0.15) has lost the tract's own layer. There the tract coasts on the mean connection of its last mmf.hold_k trusted nodes, at most mmf.coast_max voxels. On reaching trusted data again it is re-anchored if its heading is within mmf.exit_tol (1.2 rad) of the layer read there. Otherwise it stops (exit).

    Seeds start on the strongest layer of their voxel.

    Stops. The tract stops outside the volume or the propagation mask, below termination.fa_min, after termination.max_arc, and with no_lift where no corner carries a layer within 30 degrees of the heading.

    Settings of config/mmf_ml.yml¶

    key value why
    everything outside lift as the scored MMF-4h runs MMF-ml differs from MMF-4h only in the field
    lift.sparsity 0.03 F1 0.537 against 0.522 at 0.1
    lift.smooth 0 smoothing along straight lines pulls the layers of curved bundles upstream: 0.537 at 0 against 0.521 at 1 voxel
    lift.stencil fd4 the stencil of the DTI field; 0.540 against 0.537 with ls
    lift.read_min 0.125 a crossing bundle's layer is missing at scattered nodes; its continuation is read from the nodes that carry it
    lift.read_tol 30 0.509 against 0.490 at 20 degrees, which cuts real layers on bends
    mmf.hold_min 0.15 floor of the read weight below which the tract coasts; 0.541 against 0.540 at 0.25

    The F1 values in this table are single ISMRM 2015 runs that change one key at a time. They use the setup of Results with mmf_dti and mmf.hold: hold, mmf.hold_min 0.25 (except in its own row), and the other lift keys at their preset values when they were known. The read_tol pair was run with lift.stencil: ls and the code-default smoothing.

    The other lift options are experimental and off: smooth_kernel: curved, continuation, fit, debias and interp: sheet_*. Their help in docs/YAML_CONFIG.md states what each did on the data.

    Files¶

    • src/nim_calculation/nim_lift_field.m: signal → layers (section 1)
    • src/nim_tractography/nim_lift_connection.m: branches, nodal connection, the read at a point (sections 2–3)
    • src/nim_tractography/nim_field.m: builds and caches the layers for field: lift
    • src/nim_tractography/nim_tractography_mmf_ml.m: the tracker; the layers replace the DTI frame field, and the read weight replaces CL as the hold's confidence
    • src/nim_tractography/advanceFrame_ml.m: the scheme, with handles that may read the heading, f(X, T)
    • src/nim_tractography/nim_tractography_hinec.m: the streamline tracker on the layers (nearest layer to the tangent), the control above
    • config/mmf_ml.yml: the preset
    • tests/unit/TestLiftField.m, tests/fixtures/make_lift_crossing_nim.m: synthetic crossings
    • scripts/lift_field_check.py: compares the layers with the ISMRM 2015 ground-truth directions, for checking only; the tracker never reads ground truth
