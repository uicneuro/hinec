    Moving frames tractography (algorithm: mmf)¶

    nim_tractography_mmf follows a fibre as a curve that carries its own orthonormal frame. Where the streamline trackers integrate the direction field dx/ds = v(x) and re-read v from the data at every stage, the moving-frames tracker advances the whole frame E = (e1; e2; e3) with the connection 1-forms of the data's frame field, evaluated on the direction of travel, and the position with the first frame vector:

    dx/ds = e1,        dE/ds = Omega E,        Omega_ij = w_ij(e1) = sum_k wij_k e1_k.

    Omega is skew-symmetric with the two curvature components w_12(e1), w_13(e1) and the twist w_23(e1). One step is the closed-form exponential of this motion (a rotation of the frame together with a circular arc of the position, by the Rodrigues formulas), so the frame stays exactly orthonormal without re-orthogonalisation and the step is exact for a planar arc of constant curvature, at any step size. Higher order comes from Lie group integrators built around that step: Lie–Euler, the exponential midpoint rule and the commutator-free fourth-order method CF4 (integrator.method: euler | rk2 | rk4). On a fibre with torsion the tracker drops the twist (it is not a datum of DTI, see below), so there Omega is not constant along the fibre in the carried frame and the step is exact only to the order of the integrator. The scheme itself is advanceFrame.m, shared with the moving-frames test suite (the Moving Frame Tractography/matlab folder), whose notes doc/MMFtractography_notes.tex give the mathematics, the gauge, the variants and the verification on analytic fields in two and three dimensions. This page covers only what connects the scheme to the voxel data of a nim.

    The frame field and its connection, from the voxels¶

    The DTI principal eigenvector is a line field: the sign the eigensolver writes for each voxel is arbitrary. The direction between voxels is therefore read from the interpolated dyadic v1 v1', which is sign-invariant — the same six interpolants, kernel (interpolation.method) and upsample as the hinec tracker — and the data's frame at a point x is

    e1(x)   = principal axis of D(x) = interp(v1 v1')
e2, e3  = a completion of e1 (reference-axis projection)
w_1j,k  = e_j . d e1 / dx_k      j = 2, 3     (the curvature components)
w_23,k  = 0                                    (the twist is not a datum of DTI)

    By default (mmf.connection: nodal) the connection is computed at the voxel nodes from the nodal v1, by fourth-order differences of the dyadic, and interpolated between them with the kernel. With mmf.connection: derivative, d e1/dx_k is a sign-aligned central difference of e1 of the interpolated field (mmf.gradient_delta voxels), so the connection is that of the field the streamline trackers integrate. With the spline kernel that field is C2 and the connection continuous; with the cubic and trilinear kernels the derivative of the field jumps at the voxel faces, the connection is discontinuous there, and the step — exact only for a connection that is constant along the arc — is then as accurate as any integrator on a kinked right-hand side (see the numbers below). config/mmf_dti.yml therefore uses the spline kernel. The scheme runs with 'Twist' 'zero' (a rotation-minimizing normal plane), so the arbitrary completion e2, e3 never enters the position: a step uses only the curvature vector (e1 . grad) e1 of the field, expressed in the carried frame through the gauge G = E Et' that advanceFrame computes at every evaluation. That gauge is also what absorbs the sign flips of the axial data — the "flip if the dot product is negative" of streamline tracking, extended to the whole frame.

    Modes (mmf.mode)¶

    mode what the carried frame is when
    transport carried by the connection only; the data enter through the curvature and the gauge the frame scheme proper; the heading, once in error, is never corrected
    reanchor replaced by the data frame after every step (signs chosen by the transported frame) the closest relative of streamline tracking, with an arc instead of a chord; the direction cannot drift from the data
    adaptive (default) transported, re-anchored only after a step at whose end the carried e1 has drifted from the data's e1 by mmf.reanchor_tol radians or more trusts the transported heading while it agrees with the data and snaps it back where it does not

    The drift at every point and the re-anchoring decisions are recorded (meta.mmf.reanchored_fraction; per step in meta.trace with debug.trace). The default tolerance 1e-3 rad is an order of magnitude below the heading error of a one-voxel CF4 step across a sharp bend and above the step-to-step wobble of interpolated data at normal steps (see the suite's notes, section on variants).

    Termination¶

    The connection is returned non-finite wherever a point is outside the volume, outside the propagation mask (or the brain mask), below the FA floor termination.fa_min or without a well-conditioned principal axis; advanceFrame stops a tract before a step at whose stages the connection is not finite. The stages are the start of a step, its midpoint (orders 2 and 4) and its end (order 4), so with orders 1 and 2 the scheme never examines the end of a step: the tracker drops a last node that lies outside tissue. Every node of a track is therefore a valid integration node inside tissue. Leaving the volume stops the tract as well, and the cut boundary point is dropped, so tracks consist of integration nodes only, like those of the other trackers.

    The angle limit termination.angle_max (degrees per voxel of arc, through nim_angle_limit: min(angle_max * h, 90) degrees per step, none for angle_max <= 0) becomes advanceFrame's MaxTurn. It bounds two turns: the rotation h |kappa| of a step, evaluated at every stage, and the turn of the heading as the data see it — the data's e1 at the end of a step against the heading at its start (modulo sign), in every mode. In the reanchor and adaptive modes that is the turn a re-anchoring would realise, the moving-frames counterpart of the angle between consecutive directions that the streamline trackers test, which could otherwise put a kink of up to 90 degrees into a track where the data's direction changes abruptly (a crossing seen in DTI, an interface between bundles); in transport mode, which never re-anchors, it stops a tract whose heading the data contradict beyond what angle_max allows instead of letting it run through them. Where the interpolated principal axis is piecewise constant with a swap plane, as across such an interface, the connection is zero except within gradient_delta of the plane and a step whose stages straddle it sees no curvature at all; this second test is what stops the tract there. Unlike the streamline trackers, whose sign-aligned turn cannot exceed 90 degrees, the 90 degree ceiling of nim_angle_limit is a real cap here. Note also that the curvature of the interpolated principal-axis field is large wherever the dyadic is nearly degenerate (crossings, planar regions: the derivative of the principal axis grows like 1/(lambda_1 - lambda_2)), so angle_max stops more half-tracks in such regions than the node-to-node angle test of the streamline trackers does. termination.max_arc is advanceFrame's smax.

    The reasons are counted over the half-tracks of the seeds that were tracked in meta.termination (max_arc, angle, fa, outside_mask, outside, outside_domain, no_direction, and with mmf.hold coast, exit and focus, see below); an advanceFrame stop on a non-finite connection is resolved into one of these by probing the stages of the next step and their difference stencils. Seeds outside tissue or without a direction are not tracked and are counted by reason in meta.mmf.seeds_skipped. ACT masks are not used by this tracker.

    With debug.trace the per-step records of the traced seeds have the layout of the hinec tracker's (pos, dir = the carried e1, fa, h, chord, turn in degrees, flip always false, n_steps, termination), so nim_track_diagnose reads them, plus drift (angle to the data's e1), reanchored, kappa, s, coasted and confidence.

    Degenerate data: MMF-4h and MMF-4s (mmf.hold)¶

    In a voxel where two bundles cross, the single tensor is a pancake and its principal axis means nothing. A tract that reads the connection there turns into the other bundle or, more often, stops on angle_max. The hold scheme of advanceFrame lets it cross instead: inside a zone of degenerate data it does not read the data at all but coasts.

    mmf.hold scheme what the tract follows in the zone
    none (default) MMF-4a, the adaptive mode alone the data, as everywhere
    hold MMF-4h the mean connection of its last hold_k trusted nodes, in the carried frame, constant along the coast: a circle
    march MMF-4s that curvature and fan-out, marched by the flatness of the connection: exact on circles, logarithmic spirals and rays, and turning the curvature by the torsion on a helix

    The presets config/mmf_dti_hold.yml and config/mmf_dti_march.yml are mmf_dti with the scheme on, reanchor_tol: 0.2 and angle_max: 0 (see below why). See the scores below: on the ISMRM 2015 phantom the scheme halves the wrong-path streamlines at the price of shorter tracks, and most of what the presets gain in F1 comes from reanchor_tol alone.

    The zone. The confidence is the linear shape CL = (l1 - l2)/l1 of the voxel tensors (nim.eval), interpolated linearly and eroded by hold_guard voxels (the minimum over the point and 14 points at that distance); a point is in the zone where it is below hold_min. For a single bundle of eigenvalues 1.7, 0.3, 0.3 (CL 0.82) the default 0.2 is an equal crossing of about 80 degrees, 0.4 one of about 70. But CL also falls with the anisotropy of the data, so the floor belongs to the dataset: 0.15 and 0.2 were the best of the floors scored on the phantom, and on a dataset of lower anisotropy 0.2 can be the tissue itself rather than its crossings. The tracker reports the share of the tissue voxels below the floor, before the erosion (meta.mmf.zone_fraction, and on the console), and warns above 40% (mmf:holdZone). A voxel without a fit, outside the mask or below the FA floor is no data: CL is interpolated over the voxels of tissue alone and the erosion reads nothing else, so neither the edge of the mask nor the FA floor is a zone.

    Leaving it. In the adaptive and reanchor modes, a tract reaching a trusted node is re-anchored if its heading is within exit_tol of the data; otherwise the coast has left the bundle and it stops (exit). The heading test of angle_max comes first: data at the end of the step further from the heading at its start than angle_max allows stop the tract with angle. In transport mode nothing is re-anchored or tested on leaving: the tract keeps its heading and only angle_max applies. exit_tol is apart from reanchor_tol because they ask different things of the data: a test on leaving has to sit above the disagreement of neighbouring voxels. In the tissue of the sample dataset (FA at least 0.2) a quarter of the neighbouring voxels differ by more than 0.2 rad, and with the 0.2 rad of the suite's noise-free fields as the test 23% of its half-tracks ended on it. exit_delay carries the coast on for that arc beyond the edge before the test; those steps neither count towards coast_max nor reset it. A coast longer than coast_max voxels stops (coast), and so does a marched coast whose fan closes to a tenth of its cross-section, the density ten times the ring's (focus). angle_max, when it is active, applies to the coast's own rotation. The volume, the mask and the FA floor stay stops: the confidence is NaN outside tissue, which advanceFrame takes for no data, and the connection is not finite there. A seed in the zone has no trusted node and coasts straight, along the axis of the filled field described next; where that axis ties, the seed is skipped as no_direction.

    The trusted nodes must not read the zone. The held connection comes from the nodes just before it, and these are exactly where the voxel pipeline would mix the degenerate voxels in: the spline kernel is global and the jump of the axis at the edge rings over several voxels, the nodal stencil reaches two voxels, and the derivative of a jump is of the order of 1/voxel against a curvature of 0.05. With a scheme on, therefore, the principal axis of the voxels of the zone is first replaced by the one of the mean dyadic of the trusted voxels around them, from the outside in; a voxel outside the zone takes its differences from voxels outside it (central or one-sided); and the nodal tensor is continued into the zone in the same way. The last two apply to the nodal connection, the default; with mmf.connection: derivative only the axis is filled. Without these the phantoms below read 0.3 to 5 voxels instead of 0.02 to 0.35, and the march gains nothing over the hold.

    The same bundle never crosses. In trusted data two tracts of one bundle cannot cross, being integral curves of one field; two coasts can, since each follows its own held or marched connection. In the suite's crossing runs MMF-4h and MMF-4s crossed 373 and 370 pairs of same-family tracts, nearly all in the core, against 13 for the adaptive mode and none for RK4. advanceFrame therefore takes the label of each seed's bundle in two ways. 'BundleRule' stops both tracts of a bundle on a step on which they would cross while one of them coasts (reason cross). 'BundleLaw', for the march with the planar connection, takes the divergence term of the march from the neighbouring tracts of the bundle at every step, so that the coasts of a bundle march as one sheet. The tracker passes neither yet: its seeds carry no bundle labels, and a bundle would have to be defined at the entrance of each zone (the tracts entering from the same side, close together, with headings within exit_tol); the law would also need its 3-D form.

    The march in 3-D. The planar march of the suite's notes, dsigma/ds = -sigma^2 + D, dkappa/ds = -kappa sigma, has a matrix form. In the carried frame, whose normal plane is rotation-minimizing along the coast, the curvature vector a = (Omega_12, Omega_13) and the transverse block B_pq = e_p . d e1/d e_q (p, q = 2, 3) obey

    dB/ds = -B^2 + D          the flatness of the connection (the trace is Raychaudhuri's equation)
da/ds = -B' a             the curvature 1-form is closed, as div(kappa e1) = 0 says in the plane

    and the tracker takes B_0 and a_0 as the means over the ring and D = 0, so that with U = I + (s - s_0) B_0, B = B_0 U^-1, a = U^-T a_0 and u = det U, which is 1 + sigma_0 (s - s_0) on a planar field; the coast's density is 1/u of the ring's and u < 0.1 is the stop focus. The matrix is not a luxury: a closure on the trace of B would let a fan-out across the curvature change the curvature (circles in the half-planes through an axis have B = diag(0, cot(theta)/r) and constant curvature). On a helix B_0 turns a by the torsion to first order, which is all that a principal axis says about the torsion. The ring is kept in the carried frame, so a re-anchoring, which replaces the normal plane by the data's, turns the ring with it. The derivation and the checks on analytic fields are in the suite (doc/MMFtractography_notes.tex, section on the hold scheme; testadvanceFrame section 11).

    What is not supported¶

    • field: csd. The connection handles of advanceFrame are functions of position alone, and choosing an FOD peak needs the incoming tangent. Use the hinec tracker for CSD.
    • integrator.method: rkf45. The exponential step has no embedded error estimate; the tracker rejects it (mmf:integrator).

    Verification¶

    tests/unit/TestMmfTracker.m runs the tracker on the rigid-rotation phantom of TestIntegratorOrder (circles of every radius, FA 1 in an annulus) from a seed on the circle of radius 15.5, for 60 voxels of arc in each direction with h = 0.25, and on the helix of tests/fixtures/make_synthetic_nim.m (radius 15, pitch 6, constant curvature and torsion) for 40 voxels each way:

    field kernel mode integrator max distance from the true curve (voxels)
    circle spline any euler / rk2 / rk4 1.1e-5 / 6e-6 / 5e-6
    circle cubic transport euler / rk2 / rk4 3.7e-3 / 7e-4 / 7e-4
    circle trilinear transport euler / rk2 / rk4 2.0e-2 / 1.5e-2 / 1.6e-2
    circle cubic / trilinear reanchor rk4 4e-5 / 1.4e-4
    circle trilinear (Euler template) 0.48
    helix spline adaptive euler / rk2 / rk4 1.0e-3 / 3.5e-4 / 5e-6

    On the circle the exponential step is exact, so with the spline kernel the residual is the interpolation error of the dyadic field and every mode and order gives the same 1e-5; the curvature returned with the tract is 1/15.508 to seven digits and nothing re-anchors at the default tolerance (the drift stays at 4e-8 rad). With the cubic and trilinear kernels the residual of the transport mode is dominated by the integration of the connection that jumps at the voxel faces, which is why the orders separate there and why re-anchoring to the data after every step (which follows the interpolated circle instead) is a hundred times better on trilinear data; the adaptive mode keeps the transported heading on these fields (no step drifts by 1e-3 rad) and coincides with transport. On the helix the twist is dropped and the residual is the integration error, with the orders 1, 2 and 4 cleanly separated; the curvature is recovered to 4e-8.

    The test also pins the tracker contract (nim_check_tracker_output, seed lattice and order, seed_density, random jitter, min_length, a run across the chunk boundary of 4000 seeds, a seed that cannot be tracked), the straight field (a line to roundoff), the sign alignment of the connection across the flip locus of the principal axis, the precedence of propagation_mask over the brain mask, the termination reasons (max_arc, angle, fa, outside_mask, outside) with every integrator, angle_max on both the step and the data's heading (a 90-degree interface between bundles with the stages straddling the swap plane, and the crossing fixture at eight sub-voxel seed phases: every mode stops with angle and no track has a kink above angle_max), the safety ceiling when angle_max is disabled, the one-sided stencil at a volume face, a non-finite voxel in a spline field, the per-step trace and the rejection of rkf45 and csd.

    For mmf.hold it runs the phantoms with the voxels of a zone turned into pancakes, CL 0 and the axis 90 degrees off, with the presets' settings. Without the scheme every one of these tracts stops at the zone (angle):

    field zone seeds max distance from the true curve (voxels): hold / march
    circles sector of 20 degrees, 4 to 8 voxels of arc 32 phases at 4 radii 0.04 median, 0.10 worst / the same
    log spirals, 60 degrees, outwards annulus of 3 voxels, 6 of arc 24 phases at 3 radii 0.21, 0.25 / 0.02, 0.05
    the same, inwards 24 phases at 3 radii 0.09, 0.17 / 0.02, 0.07
    helix of the fixture sector of 40 degrees, 11 of arc 1, at the end of the coast 0.35 / 0.04
    crossing of the fixture the 7 voxels of band B 8 phases 0, straight to the far face / the same

    Without the filling of the zone and the one-sided stencils of the section above the circles read 1.0 and 1.5 voxels, the spirals outwards 1.2 and 1.8 for the hold and the march alike, and the helix 2.6. The test also pins that the scheme changes nothing without a zone; exit (band A turned by 30 degrees beyond the crossing, with exit_tol 0.2, and no stop with exit_tol above that); coast; focus (rays through a point in 3-D, at r/r_0 = 0.32 where the cross-section is a tenth; the hold has no fan and runs on to the FA floor); that a tract beside low-CL voxels below the FA floor or outside the mask does not coast; the ring across a re-anchoring (circles in a tilted plane, two sectors fewer trusted nodes apart than the ring holds: the hold stays in the plane to 1e-6 voxel and the march to 0.04, against 0.4 to 0.6 with the ring left behind); the FA floor and the mask inside the zone, with exit_delay as well; the share of the zone and its warning at 40%; that hold_min and hold_k reach the scheme; the signs of the axial data; the trace and the rejected inputs.

    On the sample dataset (data/parcellation_sample/sample.mat, 128 x 128 x 50, config/mmf_dti.yml at seeding.density: 1, 147k seeds of which 100k lie in tissue) the tracker produces 69.7k tracks of at least 15 voxels in about 11 minutes, every node inside the mask and no node-to-node turn above angle_max; hinec_dti.yml on the same seeds gives 74k in about 10 minutes, and for the 68.4k seeds both keep the two tracks agree to a median nearest-node distance of 0.25 voxel. The half-tracks end on the FA floor (77%), the volume faces (10%, the slab is cut at both ends), angle_max (9%, against 0.5% for hinec, for the reason given above), the mask (3%) and a missing axis (1%).

    angle_max is not needed with the scheme¶

    With the scheme on, the tract transports its frame wherever the data are degenerate, and these are the places where the data's axis turns sharply. angle_max then has little left to do, and the scheme's own exit test does that. Of the half-tracks of mmf_dti_hold on the ISMRM 2015 phantom with the angle_max of mmf_dti (45 degrees per step), 14.7% stopped on it. In a trace of 8000 seeds, 86% of those stops were steps leaving the zone whose drift (1.1 rad median) the exit test would have stopped anyway, since the heading test of angle_max runs first; 3.5% had the zone within a voxel ahead, the axis swinging before the eroded confidence falls; 10% were in trusted data with trusted data ahead. The rotation of a step never reached the limit from angle_max (0.17 rad at most). With angle_max off those stops become exits, and the scores do not move:

    angle_max streamlines valid wrong path overlap overreach mean F1 bundles mean length
    mmf_dti_hold, 225 48487 53.9% 6.1% 0.483 0.337 0.497 23 45.6
    mmf_dti_hold, 0 (the preset) 48588 53.5% 6.8% 0.483 0.339 0.497 23 46.8
    mmf_dti_march, 225 47874 53.5% 5.8% 0.476 0.325 0.495 23 45.3
    mmf_dti_march, 0 (the preset) 47979 53.2% 6.4% 0.477 0.329 0.495 23 46.5

    So the presets run with angle_max: 0. Without the scheme it is a different matter: the adaptive mode does not transport through degenerate data, it re-anchors onto them, and angle_max is the only thing that stops that snap. mmf_dti with reanchor_tol 0.2 and angle_max: 0 gives 57634 streamlines of mean length 58.5, 53.2% valid and 13.5% wrong path against 11.2%, F1 0.483 against 0.492. mmf_dti keeps its angle_max.

    MMF-4h and MMF-4s on data¶

    The ISMRM 2015 phantom has a ground truth. These rows come from the scorer of bin/run_ismrm_scoring.sh (scilpy 2.3, scil_tractogram_segment_with_ROI_and_score; point ISMRM_VENV at an environment that has it), with --save_wpc_separately for the wrong-path column. A streamline can count for several bundles there, so valid and wrong path are unique streamlines as a share of all of them, a wrong-path one being valid for no bundle. All seeds with FA at least 0.1 in the mask, termination.fa_min: 0.08, min_arc: 15, max_arc: 400:

    config streamlines valid wrong path overlap overreach mean F1 bundles mean length
    mmf_dti (reanchor_tol 1e-3) 56823 58.2% 10.3% 0.391 0.226 0.436 22 55.9
    mmf_dti with reanchor_tol 0.2 56703 54.5% 11.2% 0.509 0.405 0.492 23 54.0
    mmf_dti_hold (with angle_max 225) 48487 53.9% 6.1% 0.483 0.337 0.497 23 45.6
    mmf_dti_march (with angle_max 225) 47874 53.5% 5.8% 0.476 0.325 0.495 23 45.3

    reanchor_tol: 0.2 alone (MMF-4a in the notes) lifts F1 by far more than the scheme does, by trusting the smoother transported heading between the voxels, at the cost of more overreach (these two rows and the next were run with the angle_max of 225 that the presets have since dropped, see above). On top of it the hold halves the wrong-path streamlines (6367 to 2973, a share down by 45%) and cuts the overreach by a sixth, for 14% fewer streamlines, 16% shorter ones and 5% less overlap, so F1 hardly moves. It is a trade of recall for precision rather than a gain. The march adds nothing to the hold here: the fan-out of the last trusted nodes is a derivative of noisy data, and its gain on the phantoms above is for clean curved bundles. The settings were chosen on half the seeds, by a local reimplementation of the scorer's rules that read F1 about 0.01 lower and kept the order: hold_min 0.15 to 0.2, hold_guard 0.5, exit_tol 0.5 to 0.8 and coast_max 12 to 20 were the best of those tried, 0.15 with the higher F1 and 0.2 with fewer wrong paths; hold_min 0.25 and hold_guard 1 cost overlap. hold_min 0.2 is 39% of this phantom's tissue voxels.

    On the sample dataset there is no ground truth, and its anisotropy is low: the median CL over the tissue is 0.2, so the default floor marks 51% of the tissue voxels and the tracker warns. With a quarter of the seeds, mmf_dti with reanchor_tol 0.2 gives 17745 tracks of at least 15 voxels and a mean of 44.1; mmf_dti_hold gives 11900 and 32.5 with 49% of the steps coasted, and with hold_min: 0.1, a zone of 12.5%, 14937 and 40.0 with 10%. The fewer tracks are not a verdict either way, as the phantom shows, but a floor that coasts half of the arc is not detecting crossings. Lower it there.
